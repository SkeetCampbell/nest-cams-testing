Home Camera Server

Home Camera Server is a personal, single-user project that runs on a private computer in the owner's home. It connects to the owner's own smart cameras and doorbell so that their video can be viewed, recorded and used for motion alerts on the owner's own home server.

It is used only by its owner, to access the owner's own devices. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else, and it does not collect, sell or share data with any third party.
