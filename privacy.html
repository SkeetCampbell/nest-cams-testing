Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Home Camera Server ("the app") is a personal project used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles data.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management API to access the owner's own smart cameras and doorbell, so it can receive their live video streams and device events.

How data is used and stored

Video and device data are received on, and stored only on, a private computer in the owner's home. The app uses this data only to display, record and detect motion on the owner's own cameras. Access credentials are kept only on that computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and can delete recordings and revoke the app's access to their Google account at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
